Kitchen SOS & Rapid Fixes

🦃 Dry Turkey Breast?

Carve slices onto a warm platter, drizzle generously with hot chicken stock + melted butter, and tent tightly with foil for 8 minutes before serving.

🥣 Lumpy or Thin Gravy?

Lumpy: Pour through a fine-mesh strainer or blend for 15 seconds. Too thin: Whisk 1 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water, then simmer for 2 minutes.

🥔 Gluey Mashed Potatoes?

Fold in warm heavy cream, sour cream, or cream cheese gently with a rubber spatula—never a food processor—and top with crispy shallots to restore texture.

🔥 Oven Bottleneck?

Remember: a 15-lb turkey stays piping hot under foil + a clean kitchen towel for up to 60 minutes, freeing the entire oven for sides and rolls.
